04 · Pool Automation

One panel runs your pool, and the rest of your backyard.

We install Pentair automation so pumps, heat, lighting, chemistry, and water features work together and answer to your phone.

01
IntelliCenter

Control Panel & Load Center

The panel can serve as your load center instead of a separate breaker box, with capacity up to 150 amp. Built-in relays switch pumps, lights, and other circuits. Rated for outdoor and coastal conditions, and in the field for more than 20 years.

02
Phone Control

Run It From Anywhere

Turn equipment on and off from your phone and set schedules that run on their own. Guests visiting while you travel? Switch the slide or fountains on for them without anyone touching the panel.

03
Water Features

Waterfalls, Slides, Bubblers

Automatic actuator valves let you turn each water feature on or off remotely or on a timer. The same panel can also switch yard floodlights, ceiling fans, or any other circuit, even ones that have nothing to do with the pool.

04
Cover Card

Features That Can't Flood Your Cover

With the optional cover card, the panel knows whether the automatic cover is open. While it is closed, waterfalls, slides, deck jets, and laminars cannot be turned on, preventing an expensive mistake.

05
IntelliChem

Water Chemistry That Looks After Itself

Probes read pH and ORP in a flow cell. The controller adjusts the salt cell and injects acid when pH runs high, so the water stays balanced between visits.

06
IntelliBrite

Color Lighting From the App

Set scenes and colors from your phone or the panel. Lighting transformers are mounted outside the panel for a safe, code-friendly installation.

07
Pumps & Heat

Variable-Speed Pumps and Heater Control

Pumps connect to the panel over a simple two-wire link and run on your schedule. The panel also controls heat pumps, including heat-only and heat/cool models. Homes without full automation can still get app control through a Wi-Fi pump.

08
Always Current

Over-the-Air Updates

Once the panel is online, Pentair can send updates directly to it. A prompt appears on the panel when one is ready, and a tap installs it.

Tell us what you want to control, and we design the panel around it.

07 · Questions

Questions, answered.

What owners ask most about building a custom pool with Sebby Pools in Dallas–Fort Worth.

What areas does Sebby Pools build in?

Sebby Pools builds custom pools across Dallas–Fort Worth, Texas, including Highland Park, Preston Hollow, University Park, Southlake, Westlake, and Uptown. Tell us where your lot is and we'll tell you honestly whether we can take it on.

Do you build pools for existing homes?

Yes. Sebby Pools builds new pools for existing homes, renovates existing pools, and designs pools together with new Sebby homes. The process starts with a conversation and a walk of your site, where we read the land together before design begins.

What makes Sebby Pools different from a production pool builder?

Sebby Pools is a design-led custom builder with single accountability (one builder, one schedule, one point of contact), not a volume builder working from a catalog of fixed shapes. Design, hardscape, and budget are resolved together before construction, and the finish is held to the same standard as a Sebby home.

How long does a custom pool take?

It depends on the size and complexity of the pool, the site, and city permitting. We give you a realistic schedule during design and preconstruction, and you can follow every phase live in the six9 Builder portal.

How much does a custom pool cost?

Pricing depends on the lot, the size and shape of the pool, and the level of finish. It is resolved transparently during design and preconstruction rather than quoted from a template. A conversation and a site walk is the fastest way to a real number.

How does the Sebby Pools process work?

Five stages: Design (shape, materials, lighting, and budget as one), Engineering & Permits, Build (disciplined site leadership with regular owner updates), Finish-Out (the invisible craft of coping, tile, equipment, and light), and Handover (final walkthrough, systems training, and responsive care).

How do I start a project with Sebby Pools?

Contact founder Seb Pessy at 469-996-3789 or seb@sebby.homes. The first step is a simple conversation about your yard and what you want it to be, followed by a walk of your site in the Dallas–Fort Worth area.
